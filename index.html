Hut · About me

Zhang Wenyu 张文钰

Hi, I’m Zhang Wenyu. I graduated from Communication University of Zhejiang in Digital Media Art, specialising in interaction design, and I’m currently involved in human–computer interaction (HCI) research.

I’m curious by nature: I like observing everyday life and exploring different fields, and outside design I do photography, calligraphy and running. I’m used to finding questions worth thinking about in daily life.

Rather than chasing technical novelty for its own sake, I care more about people and needs that are easily overlooked, such as the difficulties of specific groups, emotional wellbeing, and the relationship between people and technology. I want to combine the empathy of design with the ability to build, so that good ideas don’t stay as concepts but actually improve people’s lives.

On the clothesline · Deep Sea Drifters

Fashion & material exploration · group project · The Hong Kong Polytechnic University International Summer School, Fashion and Beauty course · Jul – Aug 2025

Inspired by the ocean and the creatures in it, with jellyfish as the main reference for its flowing, transparent structure. We worked with blue organza ruffle lace, corrugated mesh, nylon net scalded with fish-scale film, pearl-wave lace and hand-kneaded paper. A basic white dress became a sea-inspired gown with a diagonal blue hem and an abstract ruffled organza top; a jellyfish hat was built from paper and iridescent fabric with a glue gun, and the second outfit was embellished with beads, rhinestones and hand-sewn fabric flowers.
